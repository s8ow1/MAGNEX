Why MAGNEX is the need of Future

Traditional manganese exploration relies on labour-intensive ground expeditions and speculative drilling campaigns that consume months, drain capital, and disturb fragile ecosystems. By integrating remote sensing and GIS analytics, MAGNEX replaces guesswork with data-driven precision, saving a lot of time, energy and most importantly our environment.

Accuracy and validation

Results depend on satellite-data quality, geological and occurrence data, feature selection, model validation and field verification. Google Earth Engine can support large-scale satellite processing while GIS provides the spatial framework.

MAGNEX is a student-led Smart India Hackathon concept focused on using space technology, GIS and AI/ML to support more targeted manganese exploration — saving time, energy, cost and, most importantly, protecting nature.
